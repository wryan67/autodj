* some features are described in the config file
* overlap means to take the last x seconds of the first song and 
overlap with the first x seconds of the next song.  volume
of first song is decreased while volume of next song is increased
* pause/resume - slow current song by reducing the tempo over the number of seconds 
identified by the variable until the song is stoped.  do the opposite when resuming

keys were identified using evtest, I'm not sure I like how that progam works,
but the identification is correct, I am using a keypad with those keys.
